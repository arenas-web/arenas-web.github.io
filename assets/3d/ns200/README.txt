ARENAS — NS200 3D

Web model based on the visually approved NS200 representation. Details are illustrative and may vary between motorcycle versions.

ns200.glb and ns200.glb.gz contain the same model; the second is gzip transport compression. Geometry, materials and embedded textures are preserved losslessly. EXT_meshopt_compression is required for both.

Runtime licenses are in assets/vendor/three and assets/vendor/meshopt.
